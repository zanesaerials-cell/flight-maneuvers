Cessna 172 3D model

This work is based on "Cessna172"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cessna172-d1b15841c29c43d0862667300bad55a4)
by KOG_THORNS (https://sketchfab.com/ioai25312),
licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made for this app: re-oriented and scaled to 172S dimensions,
origin moved to the centre of gravity, movable surfaces renamed and given
hinge pivots, textures converted to WebP and reduced, geometry compressed
with meshopt. Processing script: tools/prepare-c172s-model.mjs
